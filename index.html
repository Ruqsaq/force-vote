The Pledge

Three reforms. One vote.

We're asking every candidate for the U.S. House to publicly commit, in writing, to withhold their vote for Speaker until three long-overdue reforms get an immediate yes/no floor vote.

Term Limits

Three terms maximum for House members. Two for Senate.

Balanced Budget Amendment

A balanced federal budget, except in declared wars or national emergencies.

Congressional Stock Trading Ban

No member of Congress — or their immediate family — trades individual stocks while in office.

None of these are radical. All three poll above 70% across party lines. They've been blocked for decades because the people who'd have to pass them are the people they'd constrain. The Speaker vote is the leverage that breaks that.

How it works

The leverage is real. The mechanic is proven.

Every two years, the House elects a Speaker. In a narrow majority, even a small bloc has full power to set the terms. We're using that power for one thing.

1

The Speaker needs 218 votes.

The first vote of every new Congress is the Speaker election. No Speaker, no committees, no legislation, no business. Nothing else moves until that vote is settled.

2

A small bloc can deny it.

In January 2023, twenty holdouts forced fifteen ballots and major rule concessions before Kevin McCarthy got the gavel. The Freedom Caucus pulled the same lever in 2015. The mechanic is proven — we're just pointing it at three popular reforms instead of internal party fights.

3

Signed members force a floor vote.

Signed candidates withhold their Speaker vote until a Speaker publicly commits to an immediate yes/no floor vote on all three reforms. Not passage — just a vote. Every member of Congress goes on record. Voters see exactly who blocks what.

0 / 15
Needed for Leverage

Once 15 signed incumbents enter the next House majority, no Speaker is elected without committing to the floor vote. The actual threshold depends on the majority's margin.

What if they break it?

Every signed pledge is a public document. If a signed member votes for Speaker without securing the floor-vote commitment, they get a permanent Broke Pledge badge on this site, with their signed document published alongside their record.
